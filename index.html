農友助手

"Farmer's Assistant" — a LINE chatbot built for growers across Taiwan.

Every answer a farmer needs, inside the app they already use.

In our conversations with farmers, we kept hearing the same problem: useful information is scattered across government sites, social media, and word of mouth — and when heat waves, typhoons, pests, or floods hit, there's no time to go hunting for it. Since LINE is already Taiwan's most widely used messaging app, we built 農友助手 (Farmer's Assistant) on top of it.

✕Information scattered everywhere, hard to find in an emergency, and yet another unfamiliar app to learn

How It Works

PREDICT · PRODUCE · PROTECT

  1. 1Stress occurs — heat, salinity, pests, and more
  2. 2Sensors detect and predict the stress
  3. 3The system signals the bioreactor
  4. 4Engineered Bacillus subtilis inside begins producing a plant protectant
  5. 5The protectant is delivered to the plant's roots or leaves

Our Products

1 · Bioreactor

A one-time install beside the field or greenhouse, built for long-term use.

2 · Bacterial Cartridge

A pre-prepared, freeze-dried refill — swap in fresh B. subtilis whenever it's needed.

3 · Monitoring App

Check on crops anytime, with stress alerts and maintenance reminders.

Safely Contained

The engineered bacteria stay sealed inside the bioreactor at all times — only the natural protectant is released.

No GMOs Released

Nothing genetically modified ever enters the soil, so it's safe to use in the field or greenhouse.

Produced On-Site, On-Demand

Cuts transport and storage costs, and avoids unnecessary waste.

05農友日記

Farmer's Diary

Encourages farmers to log what they notice in the field. Over time, these records can reveal patterns in crop health and make it easy to compare one growing season against another.

  • Crop condition
  • Weather conditions
  • Irrigation
  • Pest observations
  • Plant symptoms
  • Changes after extreme weather
  • Farming practices & outcomes
DESIGN NOTE This feature reflects a core idea behind the whole platform: crop management shouldn't only react once a problem gets serious — it should also come from steady, everyday observation.
Field Journal
Farmers pick today's mood, jot down what they saw in the field, and stamp a daily check-in to build up records and points — turning consistent logging into a habit.

07未來發展

From Platform to Network

Our conversations with farmers pushed us to think beyond the features we'd already planned — 農友助手 doesn't have to be a one-way channel from us to farmers; it could also support farmers exchanging with each other.

"

In conversation with Chen Hui-Wen (陳慧文), a natural-farming practitioner, she suggested building a platform where farmers could trade seeds with one another. That reframed what 農友助手 could become: a future Seed Exchange Platform could let farmers list seeds they have on hand, find others interested in trading varieties, and help preserve and circulate locally adapted crops.
